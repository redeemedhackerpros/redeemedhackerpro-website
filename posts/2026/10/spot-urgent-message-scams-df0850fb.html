Cybersecurity reminder: Spot urgent-message scams

Keep this in mind: Scammers may use urgency, threats, prizes, or unexpected account warnings to pressure people into acting before checking the facts.

Try this: Pause and verify the request using a contact method you already trust.

Cybersecurity awareness information. Adapt advice to your device, account, and organization policies.
