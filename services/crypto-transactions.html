01 / UNDERSTAND THE RECORD

What transaction education covers

Public blockchains can expose transaction details such as transaction hashes, sending and receiving addresses, timestamps, token amounts and network fees. The exact information depends on the blockchain and the tools used to view it.

  • Identify the correct blockchain network and transaction hash (TXID).
  • Understand the difference between a wallet address, exchange account and real-world identity.
  • Read basic transaction status, timestamps, token transfers and network fees.
  • Organise records and supporting evidence for an exchange, platform or appropriate reporting authority.

What a public record does not prove

A wallet address is not automatically a person's name. A transaction explorer cannot, by itself, establish who controlled an address, why a payment was made or whether funds can be recovered. Attribution may require additional evidence and lawful processes.

Prepare useful information

Keep the transaction hash, network name, public wallet addresses, date, approximate amount, platform name and relevant correspondence. Do not share passwords, private keys or recovery phrases.

Important safety note

Never send passwords, private keys, seed phrases, one-time codes or remote-access credentials. No recovery outcome or return of funds is guaranteed.
