Recognise the warning signs before they cost you more.

Learn to identify common online fraud patterns, fake support channels, fraudulent investment offers and so-called recovery services that target people after a loss.

03 / RECOGNISE THE PATTERN

Common warning signs

  • Guaranteed investment returns or pressure to deposit immediately.
  • Requests to pay additional taxes, verification deposits or release fees before withdrawing supposed profits.
  • Support agents contacting you unexpectedly through social media or messaging apps.
  • Requests for passwords, one-time codes, private keys, seed phrases or remote access.
  • Claims that stolen cryptocurrency can be recovered with certainty or through a secret process.
  • Pressure to move a conversation away from an official platform.

What to do if you suspect a scam

Stop sending money. Preserve messages, receipts, transaction identifiers, wallet addresses and website links. Contact the relevant exchange or service using its official website or app. Consider reporting the incident to the appropriate national cybercrime or consumer-protection authority.

Beware of second-chance recovery fraud

People who have already lost money may be targeted again by individuals claiming to be investigators, lawyers, hackers or officials. Verify identities independently, insist on a written scope and never pay based on a guarantee that funds will be returned.

Important safety note

Never send passwords, private keys, seed phrases, one-time codes or remote-access credentials. No recovery outcome or return of funds is guaranteed.
