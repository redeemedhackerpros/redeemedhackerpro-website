04 / LEARN AND PREPARE

Practical learning topics

  • Password hygiene and multi-factor authentication.
  • Phishing, impersonation and social-engineering awareness.
  • Safer browsing, downloads and browser extensions.
  • Privacy settings and responsible handling of personal information.
  • Recognising suspicious payment and investment requests.
  • Basic incident documentation and reporting pathways.

Who can benefit

Individuals, families, community groups and organisations that want clearer, more practical digital-safety habits. Training scope and format should be agreed before any engagement begins.

Learning built around real decisions

Good awareness training helps people pause, verify unexpected requests, protect account access and know where to report suspicious activity. It should not rely on fear, exaggerated claims or promises that every incident can be prevented.

Important safety note

Never send passwords, private keys, seed phrases, one-time codes or remote-access credentials. No recovery outcome or return of funds is guaranteed.
