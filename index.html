A RESPONSIBLE PROCESS

Calm, careful,
evidence-first.

If you suspect a scam or account compromise, preserve information and secure accounts that remain under your control before making further payments or contacting unknown “recovery experts.”

Start with a conversation ↗
01

Listen & document

Organise the timeline, transaction IDs, public wallet addresses, messages, platform URLs and relevant receipts.

02

Review what can be verified

Separate observable facts from assumptions. A public transaction record does not usually identify a person by itself.

03

Explain realistic options

Understand platform reporting, exchange notifications, official reporting channels and account-protection steps.

04

Reduce the risk of repeat harm

Review security settings and identify practical follow-up actions. No one can guarantee the return of stolen cryptocurrency.

A NOTE ON YOUR SAFETY

Never share your seed phrase, private key or password.

Legitimate support should not need your wallet recovery phrase or one-time authentication codes. Be cautious of anyone demanding an advance payment to guarantee a recovery.
