DOCUMENTATION BEFORE CONCLUSIONS
A RESPONSIBLE PROCESS

Calm, careful, evidence-first.

If you suspect a crypto scam or account compromise, take a breath. Preserve information and secure any accounts that remain under your control before making further payments or contacting unknown “recovery experts.”

01
Listen & document

Record the timeline, transaction IDs, wallet addresses, messages, platform URLs, and relevant receipts.

02
Review what can be verified

Separate on-chain facts from assumptions. A public transaction record usually does not reveal a person's real-world identity by itself.

03
Explain realistic options

Discuss platform reporting, exchange notifications, relevant authorities, account protection, and when a qualified professional may help.

04
Protect against repeat harm

Review security settings and identify follow-up steps. No one should promise a guaranteed return of stolen cryptocurrency.

PRACTICAL KNOW-HOW

Small steps.
Stronger security.

Use these starter guides as a checklist. For active theft or threats, contact the relevant platform and official reporting channels promptly.

01 / AFTER A CRYPTO SCAM

Preserve evidence before it disappears

  • Save transaction IDs (TXIDs), public wallet addresses, dates, amounts, and network names.
  • Capture messages, emails, usernames, website addresses, payment receipts, and advertisements.
  • Contact the exchange or service involved through its official website or app.
  • Report the incident to the relevant national cybercrime or law-enforcement authority.
  • Be wary of anyone asking for an upfront fee, remote access, or a seed phrase to “unlock” a refund.
02 / ROMANCE & INVESTMENT SCAMS

Keep trust separate from money

  • Never send cryptocurrency to someone you have not independently verified, even if a relationship feels close.
  • Be cautious if someone moves conversations off-platform, avoids meeting, or introduces a “secret” investment opportunity.
  • Do not pay taxes, withdrawal fees, or verification deposits to release supposed profits without independent verification.
  • Ask a trusted person to review suspicious requests and stop sending money while you check.
03 / WALLET & ACCOUNT SECURITY

Protect the keys to your digital life

  • Never disclose a wallet seed phrase, private key, one-time code, or password to a support agent or recovery service.
  • Use unique passwords and a reputable password manager; enable multi-factor authentication where available.
  • Verify wallet downloads, browser extensions, and support contacts using official sources.
  • Keep devices and software updated, review wallet approvals carefully, and revoke suspicious token permissions using trusted tools.

CASE STUDY / 001

The investment dashboard that showed fake profits

Pattern: A site displayed growing balances but demanded additional deposits before withdrawals.

Learning: Dashboard figures are not proof that funds exist. Stop payments, save records, contact the sending platform, and report the site.

↗
CASE STUDY / 002

A romance contact introduced crypto trading

Pattern: A new online partner encouraged deposits to a platform they controlled.

Learning: Keep relationships and financial decisions separate. Verify platforms independently and never borrow or transfer money under pressure.

↗
CASE STUDY / 003

A second scam followed the first loss

Pattern: Someone claimed they could recover assets for an advance payment and sensitive wallet details.

Learning: Recovery fraud often targets people already harmed. Never share wallet secrets or pay based on guaranteed-return claims.
